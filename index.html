Isn't this just… Google?

Nope. A few quick answers.

Why not just search Google?
Google ranks by SEO — ad-stuffed sites win. GuidePost ranks by whether the guide actually helps, not by how well the site optimizes keywords.
How are guides picked?
Each guide is hand-picked by a player who actually finished the game. No automation, no algorithm — just someone who played it.
Is GuidePost free?
Yes. It's a side project built for players, not for profit.
