# 1-Minute Human Test — GuidePost Landing Page

**Why this exists:** a fresh set of eyes catches what the builder can't see. This
test proves (or disproves) that a stranger "gets it" in 20 seconds.

**The rules:** One volunteer, one minute, close to zero talking from you. Do not
explain the product first. Do not answer questions during the 60 seconds. Just
watch.

---

## How to run it

1. Open the live site on a phone or laptop the peer can touch:
   **https://issacsaleh.github.io/GuidePost/**
2. Hand it over and say exactly this:
   > *"Look at this. In your own words, what is it? What would you click?"*
3. Start a 60-second timer. **Stay quiet.** Let them scroll, click, and wander.
   Do NOT tell them what GuidePost is, even if they ask. (It's okay to say "just
   look around.")
4. When the minute is up, ask the three follow-up questions below and write down
   their answers verbatim (short notes are fine).

---

| What to notice | Yes/No | Notes |

| Naturally scrolls down / explores sections | | |
| Understands the game picker (tries the chips) | | |
| Finds a Call to Action (waitlist form) without help | | |

---

## Three follow-up questions

1. **What do you think this product is for?** *(listen: does it match "game
   guides for new players" without you hinting?)*
2. **What would you change or wish were different?** *(listen for friction,
   unclear copy, missing info, trust issues)*
3. **If this cost you nothing, would you type your email in? Why / why not?**
   *(listen for a real reason, not politeness)*
